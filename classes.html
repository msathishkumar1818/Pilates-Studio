Studio Etiquette

Preparing for Your Studio Session

Help us maintain a peaceful, hygienic sanctuary by reviewing our standard arrival recommendations.

01

Arrive 10–15 Minutes Prior

Allows time to check in at reception, secure personal items in lockers, and hydrate before the carriage warms up.

02

Grip Socks Are Mandatory

For hygiene and foot stability on the carriage footbar. Premium studio grip socks are available at the front desk.

03

Inform Instructor of Current Injuries

Notify your educator before class so they can provide spring modifications or headrest adjustments seamlessly.
